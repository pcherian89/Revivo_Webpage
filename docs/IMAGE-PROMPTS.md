# Revivo website — image plan and ChatGPT prompt pack

Images for revivosportech.com, generated in ChatGPT and placed into the site by Claude.
Everything here follows `CLAUDE.md`: no fake software screens, no invented clients or logos, no real faces, no
ECG or hospital looks, no robots or blue/purple "AI" visuals. Images are illustrative mood pictures, never
"our work".

---

## 1. Where images go

| # | Place on the site | What it is | Shape to generate | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero — inside the signal circle on the right** | A round "window" inside the circles where the moving line arrives. The line runs into it and the image glows faintly when the light reaches it. | Square 1:1 (1024×1024) | Recommended |
| 2–5 | **The four areas** (Events & Operations, Growth & Engagement, Athletes & Performance, Management & Reporting) | When a visitor opens an area, a wide, faint image fades in behind that area's four capabilities. On phones, a slim strip at the top of the opened area. | Landscape 3:2 (1536×1024), wide composition | Recommended |
| 6–21 | **The 16 capability panels** | A cinematic image across the top of each detail panel, fading into the panel below. This is where images add the most. | Landscape 3:2 (1536×1024) | Essential |
| 22–25 | **How Revivo works** (Discover, Define, Prototype, Build) | Small, quiet images under each step that brighten as the running light passes. | Landscape 3:2 | Optional |
| 26 | **/contact page** | A wide band above the heading, fading into black. | Landscape 3:2 | Optional |
| 27 | **Link preview card** (what shows on WhatsApp / LinkedIn) | Image on the right, logo and tagline on the left. | Landscape 3:2 | Recommended |

**Deliberately left without images:** the headline area itself (it must stay instantly readable), the
"What is your organisation not seeing yet?" form section (calm, so people fill the form), Privacy and Terms.

**The lime line is added by the website, not by ChatGPT.** Generated "neon" lines usually look cheap and the
colour is never exact. Ask ChatGPT for dark, calm images with **no lime at all**; the site draws Revivo's own
thin lime signal line across or into each image, so all 27 images connect to the brand in exactly the same way.

## 2. How to make them (step by step)

1. Open a **new chat** in ChatGPT.
2. Paste the **Master style prompt** (section 3) and send it. Wait for ChatGPT to confirm.
3. Generate image **#1 (hero)** first. Ask for 2–3 versions and pick the best.
4. Say: **"Use this image as the style reference for every image that follows in this chat."**
5. Generate the rest **in the same chat**, one prompt at a time (section 4). If one drifts, reply:
   *"Closer to the approved reference: darker, calmer, less colour, no text."*
6. Check each image against the **reject list** (section 5) before keeping it.
7. Download each one at full size (PNG is fine) and name it as shown in section 6.
8. Upload them to Claude. Claude will crop, compress, colour-match and place them, then send you laptop and phone
   screenshots before anything goes live.

## 3. Master style prompt (paste this first)

> For this whole conversation you are creating one consistent series of images for Revivo, a company that
> designs custom digital and AI systems for sports organisations around the world. Every image must look like
> a still from a calm, premium documentary about how sport really runs behind the scenes.
>
> STYLE: cinematic editorial photography. Real places, real light, real materials. Night, dusk, dawn or
> after-hours interiors. One soft, low side light or back light; deep shadows that fall almost to pure black
> (#0A0A0B); highlights a warm off-white (#F5F5F2). Muted, low-saturation colour grade, close to monochrome,
> with fine film grain. 35–50mm lens feel, shallow depth of field, eye-level or straight-overhead viewpoints.
> At least 40% of every frame is quiet dark space. Compositions are simple, precise and uncluttered.
>
> PEOPLE: only hands, backs, silhouettes or soft out-of-focus figures. No face is ever sharp or recognisable.
> Vary ages, genders, skin tones and abilities, and show many roles: organisers, venue crew, volunteers,
> administrators, fans, coaches, officials, community participants — not only elite athletes.
>
> SPORTS: vary across the series — football, cricket, basketball, hockey, athletics, swimming, rugby, netball,
> badminton, tennis, boxing, gyms and fitness clubs, academies, community grounds, wheelchair sport.
>
> NEVER INCLUDE: any text, letters, numbers or signage; logos, crests, team kits, sponsor marks or jersey
> numbers; real or recognisable stadiums; screens, phones or laptops showing any interface, chart or dashboard
> (turn screens away or keep them dark); trophies; scoreboards; flags; robots, brains, circuit boards,
> holograms, particles, glowing orbs or glass shapes; blue or purple light; neon; lime or yellow-green glow;
> heart-rate or ECG lines; hospital or medical settings; glossy "AI-looking" skin or plastic surfaces.
>
> Confirm you understand, then wait for my first image request.

**Add this line to the end of every request:**
> Match the master style and the approved reference exactly. No text, no logos, no faces, no lime.

## 5. Reject an image if…

- hands or limbs look wrong (extra or merged fingers, bent arms)
- **any** text, numbers, letters, signage or jersey numbers appear, even garbled
- logos, crests, sponsor marks, team colours or a recognisable stadium appear
- a face is sharp or realistic, or anyone looks like a real person
- a screen shows anything (interface, chart, dashboard)
- there is lime/yellow-green glow, neon, or blue/purple light
- it has ECG/heartbeat lines or anything medical
- surfaces look plastic or "AI-glossy"; balls, nets or equipment are warped
- the dark space asked for (bottom third, left half, etc.) is filled

## 7. How Claude will place them (for reference)

- One colour grade applied to every image so the whole set matches, then compressed (AVIF/WebP) and loaded only
  when needed, so the site stays fast.
- Every image fades into the black background; text never sits on busy detail.
- Revivo's lime signal line is drawn by the site across each image: into the hero circle, along the area bands,
  under each capability image, through the process images.
- Images stay still (no zooming or sliding); the running light remains the only motion.
- Screenshots on laptop and phone are sent for approval before anything goes live.
